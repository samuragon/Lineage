Import organisation data ›
Expects { roles:[...], employees:[...] }. Missing fields (manager, headcount, title, occupant) are kept and flagged as data gaps rather than rejected.
One row per year-record. First column must be role or employee. Malformed rows are skipped and listed under Data Gaps — they never crash the import.
Unstructured lines are parsed heuristically (year, name, department keywords, "reports to X"). Anything that can't be confidently parsed is logged as a data gap and skipped, not thrown away silently.
